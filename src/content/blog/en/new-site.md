This site used to live in a page builder that loaded cookies and a third-party monitoring service. That didn't fit what I stand for: if I talk about anti-surveillance, the least I can do is not watch the people who read me.

It is now a static site, published from a GitHub repository, with no cookies, no analytics and no fonts or images served by third parties.

From now on this blog is where working notes on overtourism, territory, invisible labour and digital ecosystems will go. You can also follow it by [RSS](/en/rss.xml) or on [Mastodon](https://mastodon.art/@juanalbglz).
